Matching prefers a member_id column when both files have one; otherwise it matches by normalized name + date of birth, and flags anything ambiguous below rather than guessing silently. Per-test deltas use each test's uncapped Z-score and uncapped age-equivalent (MAE) — not the 21–95, cap-limited values shown on the printed report — so real improvement or decline at the very top or bottom of the scale isn't hidden by the display floor/ceiling. A test flagged with * hit the ±2.5 Z-score cap used in the composite, meaning the true change may be larger than shown. This runs entirely in your browser; no file is uploaded anywhere.
